# Commercial use

nykma is free for personal and noncommercial use under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). Any use by or for a company needs a paid commercial licence.

## Free, no licence needed

- Making ads for yourself, your hobby project or your own learning
- Studying the code, changing it and sharing your changes for noncommercial use
- Charities, schools, public research and government bodies, which the licence names as noncommercial

## Needs a commercial licence

- A company using nykma, at any size, including a one-person company or a freelancer working for clients
- Making ads for a product that earns money, whether it's yours or a client's
- Selling, hosting or bundling nykma or a changed version of it

If you're not sure, ask. The answer is usually quick.

## Buying a licence

Contact sales through the form at [nobodyknowsmyapp.com/pricing](https://nobodyknowsmyapp.com/pricing), or email sales@nobodyknowsmyapp.com. Say who you are, the company, and how many people will use nykma, and you'll get a price by email. A licence covers one company and every person in it.

## Things nykma uses that have their own terms

The commercial licence covers nykma's code. These services and models are licensed separately, by you, with their owners:

- **ElevenLabs:** voices and music made with it need a paid ElevenLabs plan for commercial use.
- **LTX-2.5:** `bun run local:setup` downloads it to your machine under Lightricks' own licence.
- **OpenRouter models, Pexels and Pixabay:** each has its own terms for what you make with them.
- **Fonts** in `brands/` and `public/fonts/` are under the SIL Open Font License, which allows commercial use.
